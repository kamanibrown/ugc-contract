02 / Terms

The terms, in full.

These standard terms apply to every UGC engagement between the Creator and the Brand, unless a signed Deal Schedule states otherwise for that deal.

1

Parties

The Creator is Kamani Brown. The Brand is the company named in the Deal Schedule for each booking. These standard terms apply to every UGC engagement unless a signed Deal Schedule states otherwise for that deal.

2

Deliverables

Deliverables are as set out in the package booked: a Single Video (one UGC video), a 3-Video Test Pack (three UGC videos with different hooks / angles), or a Monthly Content Bank (the monthly batch stated in the deal). Each video is delivered in the format, length and caption versions described at booking, by the delivery date agreed before filming.

3

Payment

3.150% of the fee is payable upfront to book the work, with the balance payable Net-15 (within 15 days of delivery / invoice).

3.2Overdue balances carry a late fee of 2% per month.

3.3All fees are in USD and are net to the Creator: payment-processing fees are on the Brand or grossed up, so the full quoted fee clears. Payment details are completed privately at signing and never appear on the public page.

3.4Payment is by bank transfer (Wise), which is preferred. PayPal and Payoneer are also available where the Deal Schedule states it for that booking; where PayPal is used, its processing fees are grossed up under clause 3.3 so the full quoted fee clears in USD.

4

Revisions

Two revision rounds are included in the standard terms, covering pacing, caption and call-to-action adjustments within the agreed brief. An extra revision round is billed per the UGC rate sheet. Changes that amount to a new concept or a re-shoot are quoted as new work.

5

Usage & Licence

Organic use is included in the base fee — the Brand may post the video on its own channels and product pages. Paid advertising usage, whitelisting, and Spark / paid amplification require the paid usage add-on and run only for the licence period stated in the Deal Schedule. Use beyond that period, or in paid media without the add-on, is not licensed under the base fee.

6

Raw Footage

Raw footage is only supplied when it is agreed in the Deal Schedule. Where it is not agreed, the Brand receives the finished deliverables only.

7

Portfolio Rights

The Creator retains the right to show the work in his portfolio and channels as an example of his craft, unless the deal states a specific hold or exclusivity window that applies.

8

Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of New Brunswick, Canada.
